OUR APPROACH

Foundation, then partnership

Every engagement begins with a foundation period that is focused, intensive, and tailored to the household. We map the operating picture, build the systems, and establish the rhythms that will run the partnership forward.

From there, the work transitions into an ongoing partnership. Communication is async by design. We meet on a cadence that fits you.

Engagements are scoped individually. Pricing and structure are shared during the discovery call.

HOW WE OPERATE

Principles

Discretion

We operate inside highly personal environments with the professionalism, judgment, and confidentiality expected in high-trust advisory roles.

Ownership

We take responsibility for outcomes, not just coordination, proactively managing details, dependencies, and follow-through across the household.

Continuity

We build systems that hold as life evolves, reducing friction, rework, and the mental burden of constantly keeping everything in motion.

Liza Kaskey, Founder

The founder

Before founding Kaskey Consulting, Liza Kaskey advised institutional clients in investment banking across complex, high-stakes transactions involving dozens of stakeholders, advisors, and parallel workstreams.

The work required constant coordination across legal, financial, operational, and executive teams, managing sensitive processes where discretion, organization, and proactive execution were critical.

That operational discipline became the foundation for Kaskey Consulting's approach to household management.
